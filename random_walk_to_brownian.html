Mathematical Explanation

This animation demonstrates Donsker's Theorem (Functional Central Limit Theorem), which shows how random walks converge to Brownian motion.

Sn(t) = S⌊nt⌋ / √n → B(t) as n → ∞

Key Points:

  • Space Scaling: Divide position by √n to normalize variance
  • Time Scaling: Take n times as many steps in the same time interval
  • Convergence: As n increases, the scaled random walk approaches Brownian motion
  • Universal Limit: The limit is the same regardless of the step distribution (±1 here)
